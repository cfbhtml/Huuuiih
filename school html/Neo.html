Born in Venice in 1678, Vivaldi became one of the Baroque era’s most dazzling musical minds. A virtuoso violinist and prolific composer, he shaped the concerto into a thrilling conversation between soloist and orchestra. His music still feels alive: brilliant, theatrical, and full of movement.

The Four Seasons

Four violin concertos that paint the year in sound: a springtime chorus, summer thunder, autumn dancing, and winter’s icy bite. First published in 1725, they remain among the most recognized works in classical music.
